ABOUT RELVIX STUDIO

Our Story

RELVIX STUDIO was founded by Nandini Kannan with a simple mission: to create games and stories that leave a lasting impression. What started as a passion project has grown into a creative hub for game development and interactive storytelling.

What RELVIX means

REL stands for Relentless - our drive to keep creating, keep improving, and never give up. VIX stands for Victory - the triumph of every idea that comes to life.

Our Vision

We believe every game should tell a story, and every story should feel like a game. That's why we built RELVIX GAMES and RELVIX UNIVERSE - two sides of the same creative coin.
