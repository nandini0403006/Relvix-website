Fairytale

Are you a fairytale?
because i love you
even when it takes me to the hell,
even when it takes me to the heaven,
even when it hurts me,
even when it is sweet to me,
even when it is crazy to me,
even when it is hard to me,
even when i cannot fine it,
even when i could see it,
even when i could not live it...

Longing

longing is where people miss,
longing is where people become,
longing is where people feel loneliness,
longing is where everything changes,
longing is where you see yourself...
